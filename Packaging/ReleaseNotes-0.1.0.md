# AI Limit Notch 0.1.0

First public preview of AI Limit Notch for macOS.

- Compact 20 px Notch display with 1–3 providers
- Claude Code and Codex usage-limit monitoring
- Expandable 5-hour, session, and weekly limit details
- Color and Black & White themes
- Thai and English settings
- Launch at Login and Sparkle update support
- Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs

This preview is signed with an Apple Development certificate but is not notarized with Developer ID. macOS may ask you to allow the app from **System Settings › Privacy & Security** on first launch.
