# AI Limit Notch 0.1.4

This update adds adjustable transparency for the compact Notch.

- Add a Mini opacity slider in Notch settings
- Adjust the mini background from 20% to 100% in 5% steps
- Keep provider logos, usage values, and loading indicators fully legible
- Keep the expanded Notch fully opaque
- Persist the selected opacity across app launches

The app is signed with an Apple Development certificate but is not notarized with Developer ID. macOS may ask you to allow it from **System Settings › Privacy & Security** on first launch.
